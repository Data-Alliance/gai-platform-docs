# What is gcube?

> A **GPU sharing cloud platform** that connects developers who need GPU power with providers who have idle GPUs to spare.

---

## Why gcube

High-performance GPUs are essential for AI model training, deep learning inference, and video rendering.  
Yet public cloud costs remain prohibitive — while idle GPU capacity sits unused across the globe.

gcube solves both problems at once.

- **Consumers** — Cut GPU costs by up to 90% compared to traditional cloud providers
- **Providers** — Monetize idle GPUs and generate additional revenue

---

## Core technology

### Heterogeneous GPU Pooling
GPUs from different manufacturers and models (RTX 3090, H100, etc.) are unified into a single managed pool.  
Choose the right GPU for each workload with full flexibility.

### Container-Based Multi-Tenancy
Container isolation allows multiple users to share the same GPU simultaneously — securely.  
Data security and environment consistency are guaranteed across all tenants.

### Blockchain-Based Fair Compensation
A patented distributed contribution tracking system ensures transparent, fair payouts to every GPU provider.
