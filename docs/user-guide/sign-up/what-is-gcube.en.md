# **What is gcube?**

## **Key Concepts to Know Before Getting Started**

gcube is a GPU sharing cloud platform that connects providers who have idle GPUs with consumers who need GPU resources.

---

## **Problems gcube Solves**

High-performance GPUs are essential for AI development, video rendering, and deep learning training — but existing cloud services come with significant costs.
At the same time, many people own high-performance GPUs that sit idle most of the time.

gcube solves both problems at once.

- **Provider:** Share idle GPUs to generate additional revenue
- **Consumer:** Reduce GPU costs by up to 90% compared to traditional cloud services

---
